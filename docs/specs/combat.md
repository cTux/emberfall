# Combat, enemies and rewards

Status: implemented baseline. Goal: Responsive class combat with authoritative damage, cooperative scaling and clear threats. See [technical design](../design/simulation.md).

## COMBAT-01 — Attacks and ailments

Warrior keeps its 5-damage slash. Ranger, Mage, and Druid each launch two base-spell projectiles aimed around the cursor or nearest auto-target, with 6 degrees between adjacent projectiles. The shared spread formula centers odd counts on the aim (three: -6, 0, +6 degrees) and even counts around it (two: -3, +3; four: -9, -3, +3, +9 degrees). Projectiles maintain their launch direction without homing. Ranger arrows deal 5 damage and pierce with a 1000-unit travel limit. Mage fireballs travel up to 250 units with auto-target enabled; each impact deals 3 direct damage and explodes in a 100-unit radius for 1 damage to other enemies. The direct target receives no explosion damage from that projectile. The existing 0.7-second attack cadence is retained. Bleed, poison, and burn retain their 10% proc chance, uncapped stacks, 1 damage per stack per second, and shared five-second expiry refreshed by new stacks. Damage over time does not trigger further ailments; the latest applying player receives kill credit for that ailment.

Druid launches two root projectiles with a 250-unit travel limit when auto-target is enabled. Each hit deals 3 damage immediately and adds one root stack. The first stack immobilizes for 3 seconds; each subsequent active stack adds 3 seconds to the remaining duration. Expired roots restart at one stack and 3 seconds. Roots deal no damage over time. Rooted ordinary enemies cannot move; bosses show stacks and take impact damage but remain mobile. Root kills use the shared XP, loot, and boss-completion paths.

Sword aim tracks the closest living enemy every rendered frame and server tick, rotating along the shortest arc with shared 80 ms exponential smoothing (about 240 ms to complete 95% of a turn). Both hit detection and the green zone use this smoothed angle. During each 260 ms active swing (700 ms cooldown), the server checks every enemy collision body against the green half-disc, including radius and diameter edges and forest wrapping. An enemy can take 5 damage only once per swing, even if it enters late or aim changes. The green pulse and sword animation use the server swing phase rather than an independent attack timer.

## COMBAT-02 — Encounters and scaling

Enemies target the nearest living forest player using wrapped distances. Shared deterministic steering routes around nearby trunks, and a wrapped spatial grid separates bodies according to their radii (8–16 units). Every tenth successful spawn is an elite (50 HP, yellow health bar); normal enemies have 10 HP before archetype and party modifiers. Each additional player in the forest multiplies enemy HP by 1.75 and dropped XP by 1.2: with N scene members, the multipliers are `1.75 ** max(0, N - 1)` and `1.2 ** max(0, N - 1)`. Three players give 3.0625 times HP and 1.44 times dropped XP. Village players and companions do not count; dead scene members do. Existing enemies and pending spawns adjust on membership changes while retaining their health percentage; new spawns, elites and bosses use the same HP multiplier. XP drop amounts are fixed at death, shared with every living scene player on collection, and saved without rounding; the existing 1 XP kill credit is unchanged. Skeletons move at 64 units/second, small slime runners at 104, large bear brutes at 40, and ranged eye casters at 54. All hits deal 10 damage with a one-second per-player damage cooldown. Melee windups are 500–1000 ms: a ground circle fills red before damage, and targets outside its range are not hit. Missed attacks resume chasing. Casters stop within 210 units, wind up for 900 ms and fire red projectiles at 210 units/second toward the marked location. Projectiles use swept substeps, wrap, collide with trees and players, and expire after 2.5 seconds. All enemy spawns, including bosses, have a one-second red zone before becoming solid; occupied spawns wait until their space clears. The server owns attacks, damage, deaths, votes, membership and deadlines. Enemy spawning is bounded at 160 per scene; network snapshots are still full snapshots, not a large-horde scalability claim.

Non-boss spawns roll once: 10% brute (3x HP), 10% caster (0.7x HP), otherwise regular skeleton/fast melee. Modifiers multiply normal/elite HP; boss base HP remains 200 before party scaling. Debuff icons and stack counts appear above enemy health bars. All classes use distinct supplied character sprites, walking and attack animations. No talent gameplay is introduced yet.

## COMBAT-03 — Bear

Each Druid has a permanent companion named Bear in village and forest, with its own health bar and 1.5x the player's maximum HP. Bear targets and chases the nearest living enemy within 200 units of its owner, using the warrior's 88-unit forward slash geometry, 260 ms active window and 700 ms cadence, but claws deal 2 damage once per enemy per swing. Bear moves at 1.3x player speed (234 units/second) for chasing, returning, and dodging in both areas. Bear approaches an 80-unit gap instead of the enemy center, retreats to restore that spacing when enemies close in, and prioritizes escaping telegraphed attack circles and sidestepping projectiles predicted to pass within 30 units in the next 0.35 seconds. Bear chooses its target and movement destination once per second, then moves smoothly toward that point; short warnings between decisions, hazards, and blocked escape routes can still damage Bear. Movement retains tree avoidance, and its walking animation plays only while it moves. Beyond 200 units of wrapped distance from its owner it stops attacking and returns; within 20 units it resumes hunting on its next decision. Beyond 500 pixels it immediately teleports to its owner, using wrapped distance in both areas. Enemies can target and damage Bear with melee and projectiles; death schedules full-health resurrection at the owner exactly five seconds later. A dead owner suspends hunting. Companion state is temporary, server-owned Player state, interpolated in snapshots and repositioned on area transfers; character saves continue to persist class progress, with fresh Druid progress added automatically to older saves.

## COMBAT-04 — Training

The lobby has two marked training clearings: one skeleton dummy on the west side and six in a 1/2/3 triangle on the east side. Each has 1,000,000,000 HP, replenished to full every second by the server. Dummies never move or attack and award no XP or loot. Player attacks, ailments, projectiles, and the companion use the same combat handling as the forest. The unlabeled areas each have a 135-unit horizontal radius and a 114.75-unit vertical radius. Lobby auto-attacks and companion hunting start only while the player's feet are inside either area and stop on leaving; already-fired projectiles and ailments can finish. Forest combat is unchanged. Training state is shared within each world and separate from the forest scene.

## COMBAT-05 — Rewards and damage accounting

Combat presentation includes sword sparks, enemy attack sparks and short red blood bursts for damaged players and enemies. Killed enemies drop a glowing, gently hopping experience orb and independently have a 10% chance to drop a gold coin. Server-owned drops are collected within 22 units after a 300 ms initial hop; collection removes them for everyone and each experience orb grants its death-time amount (1 XP solo, multiplied by the party XP factor) to every living forest player, regardless of distance. Dead players and players in the lobby receive no pickup XP; gold grants no currency yet (existing kill XP is unchanged). Drops remain collectible after boss defeat, expire after 60 seconds, and are capped at 512 per scene. Glow textures are cached, offscreen drops are culled, and blood rendering is limited to 80 recent hit events.

Experience shards and coins attract after the initial hop toward the nearest living forest player within 140 units, moving at 280 units/second. XP pickup rewards all living scene players. Separately, the credited killer receives 1 XP at death; Bear damage credits its owner. Training grants neither reward. Gold is a visible pickup without a currency balance or spending system.

DPS is actual enemy damage over the previous five seconds divided by five, including ailments and Bear and excluding overkill. It returns to zero after five seconds without damage.

## Acceptance

- A Warrior swing hits each overlapping enemy at most once during its active window, including late entrants and bodies across a seam.
- Ranged classes fire two straight projectiles with centered spread. Fireball splash excludes its direct target; roots extend remaining duration without immobilizing bosses.
- Ailments stack and refresh expiry without recursively applying ailments. Latest applicator receives ailment kill credit.
- Three forest members produce 3.0625× enemy HP and 1.44× dropped XP; village members and Bear do not count, dead scene members do. Membership changes preserve enemy health percentage.
- Telegraphs precede spawns and attacks; moving outside melee range avoids a hit. Boss death alone completes the encounter.
- Bear obeys chase/return/teleport boundaries, takes damage and resurrects after five seconds; a dead owner suspends hunting.
- Training uses the same attack paths, starts new attacks only inside its zones, and grants no XP or loot.
- Pickup removes a drop for everyone; dead/village players gain no pickup XP and fractional XP survives saving.
